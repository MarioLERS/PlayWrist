PlayWrist – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

PlayWrist is a personal app provided by Jordi Esbri Corbella ("we", "us") that lets you control, from your HUAWEI smartwatch, the Spotify app that is playing music on your phone. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. PlayWrist has no server of its own.

To work, the watch app and the PlayWrist phone app exchange short messages over the Bluetooth connection managed by Huawei Health (HUAWEI Wear Engine): the watch sends commands (play/pause, next/previous, volume, like, add to playlist, browse) and the phone replies with the title and artist of the current song and the playback state. This information is only shown on your watch and is not kept.

The watch app only keeps, while it is open, which screen you are on (app settings and running status).

2. Third-party services

The PlayWrist phone app talks to Spotify on your behalf, using Spotify's official SDK and Web API with the Spotify account you sign in with. Spotify's own privacy policy applies to that data: spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy. The connection between the watch and the phone is provided by Huawei Health and HUAWEI Wear Engine.

3. Storage and retention

No personal information is sent to or stored on any server operated by us. Data stays on your watch and phone. The Spotify access token used by the phone app is stored encrypted on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app or sign out.

4. Your rights and contact

For any question or request about your privacy, contact us at jordi_esbri@hotmail.com.
